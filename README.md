### Sakura-Report

## Objective
The objective of this room is to test a variety of different OSINT techniques and can be completed by beginners.

## Skills Gained
- Image and file analysis: decoding binary text and inspecting image source code or metadata for clues.
- Username and account correlation: tracing a username across platforms and linking related social-media accounts.
- Public key analysis: examining a PGP key to uncover associated contact information.
- GitHub investigation: reviewing repositories and commit history for exposed clues, including cryptocurrency wallet details.
- Blockchain analysis: using a blockchain explorer to trace wallet transactions, identify a mining pool, and spot other cryptocurrencies.
- Wireless-network research: connecting a Wi-Fi SSID to a BSSID and mapping its location.
- Geolocation and travel research: interpreting social-media photos, captions, maps, landmarks, and flight clues to identify airports and locations.
- Evidence correlation: combining separate findings to build a more complete picture of a target.

## Tools Used
- TryHackMe
- PicPick Version 7.4.0

## Required Flags
- What username does the attacker go by?
- What is the full email address used by the attacker?
- What is the attacker's full real name?
- What cryptocurrency does the attacker own a cryptocurrency wallet for?
- What is the attacker's cryptocurrency wallet address?
- What mining pool did the attacker receive payments from on January 23, 2021 UTC?
- What other cryptocurrency did the attacker exchange with using their cryptocurrency wallet?
- What is the attacker's current Twitter handle?
- What is the BSSID for the attacker's Home WiFi?
- What airport is closest to the location the attacker shared a photo from prior to getting on their flight?
- What airport did the attacker have their last layover in?
- What lake can be seen in the map shared by the attacker as they were on their final flight home?
- What city does the attacker likely consider "home"?
